# CDLTestready keywords

Updated: 2026-10-13. Plain Markdown source of truth for the proposed `cdl_keywords` CRUD tool. The tool and email automation are not implemented or enabled yet. Earlier research is preserved in `docs/seo/INITIAL_KEYWORD_RESEARCH.md`.

Mission: help people prepare confidently for ALL CDL knowledge and skills exams, primarily in Texas and also throughout the United States, in English and Spanish. Study guidance supports preparation and does not replace required training or supervised driving practice. No guaranteed passing claims.

These are researched seed queries and natural Spanish query variants, not measured search-volume or keyword-difficulty rankings. Each row represents one bilingual topic cluster; related queries belong in the same row/page when their intent is the same. English spelling is “air brakes”; do not create a separate thin page for the misspelling “airbreaks.”

## State coverage and selection

Support all 50 states plus DC. Start with Texas. Proposed daily mix after the first core batch: three Texas topic pairs, one other-state pair, and one nationwide pair. Rotate the other-state slot; adjust based on reader requests and actual search data. This mix is a planning default, not an activated schedule.

State coverage queue: AL, AK, AZ, AR, CA, CO, CT, DE, FL, GA, HI, ID, IL, IN, IA, KS, KY, LA, ME, MD, MA, MI, MN, MS, MO, MT, NE, NV, NH, NJ, NM, NY, NC, ND, OH, OK, OR, PA, RI, SC, SD, TN, TX, UT, VT, VA, WA, WV, WI, WY, DC.

For any state, research these templates in both languages: `[state] CDL general knowledge practice test`, `[state] CDL air brakes practice test`, `[state] CDL vehicle inspection`, and the other exam areas above. Create an explicit registry row when selected for research. Templates are discovery prompts, not instructions to mass-publish 51 near-identical pages. Source each state's actual differences before writing a state article. When no meaningful distinction exists, improve the nationwide resource instead.

## Coverage strategy

Keep general knowledge, air brakes, and vehicle inspection as recurring core priorities. Cover commercial rules where applicable, combination vehicles, all endorsement subjects, basic vehicle control, and road-test preparation in the rotation. Build confidence through explanations, original practice scenarios, vocabulary, and realistic readiness checks.

Target each intent through the appropriate existing page, an approved update, or a genuinely distinct article. Existing live air-brake and pre-trip pages mean new keyword rows do not automatically justify new URLs. Inspect the full current article inventory before choosing new slugs. Do not claim that a practice test or interactive feature exists unless it actually works on the website.

## Official source leads

- [Texas DPS application and testing overview](https://www.dps.texas.gov/section/driver-license/how-do-i-apply-commercial-driver-license): authoritative Texas test categories and current testing-language policy. Spanish content is study support; do not imply that Spanish-language preparation means a Spanish official exam is available.
- [Texas commercial handbook](https://www.dps.texas.gov/Internetforms/getForm.ashx?id=DL-7C.pdf) and [Spanish handbook](https://www.dps.texas.gov/internetforms/getForm.ashx?id=DL-7CS.pdf): obtain the current editions from DPS and check for differences; older translated material must not override newer official requirements.
- [Texas CDL FAQ](https://www.dps.texas.gov/section/driver-license/faq/commercial-driver-license-cdl-frequently-asked-question).
- [FMCSA CDL overview](https://www.fmcsa.dot.gov/registration/commercial-drivers-license/drivers).

Check sources at drafting time, record jurisdiction and checked date, and verify the current test system for the relevant testing location. Competitor search results inform query wording only; they do not establish safety rules or official exam formats.
