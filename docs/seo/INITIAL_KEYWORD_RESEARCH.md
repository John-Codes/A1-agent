## First five review candidates

These are briefs, not finished 800-word articles. Recheck sources and live inventory when generation begins.

### 1. Do You Need ELDT Before a CDL Permit?

- Primary query: `do I need ELDT before CDL permit`.
- Proposed slug: `eldt-before-cdl-permit`.
- Reader outcome: understand when training verification happens for their license/endorsement and why a state's process must be checked.
- Outline: short answer with federal/state distinction; CLP versus skills-test timeline; first-time H/P/S considerations; where to verify training; three original scenario questions; next steps.
- Original value: a decision table separating CLP knowledge tests, CDL skills tests, and H endorsement knowledge tests.
- Source leads: [FMCSA CDL steps](https://www.fmcsa.dot.gov/registration/commercial-drivers-license/how-do-i-get-commercial-drivers-license), [FMCSA ELDT](https://www.fmcsa.dot.gov/registration/commercial-drivers-license/entry-level-driver-training-eldt), [Texas official CDL FAQ](https://www.dps.texas.gov/section/driver-license/faq/commercial-driver-license-cdl-frequently-asked-question).
- Internal links: `/blog/cdl-vs-clp-difference/`, `/practice-tests/general-knowledge/`.
- Guardrail: do not claim that this website provides qualifying ELDT or that one state's sequence applies everywhere.

### 2. CDL Coupling and Uncoupling: What to Understand

- Primary query: `CDL coupling and uncoupling`.
- Proposed slug: `cdl-coupling-uncoupling-study-guide`.
- Reader outcome: understand coupling components, inspection logic, and common knowledge-test confusions.
- Outline: component glossary; why each check matters; comparison of coupling versus uncoupling; common mistakes; original questions with answer explanations; official-manual reading assignment.
- Original value: a component/function/check table, with a simple original diagram if it genuinely helps.
- Source lead: [California official handbook portal](https://www.dmv.ca.gov/portal/driver-handbooks/); select its current commercial handbook and combination-vehicle section before drafting. Search results include outdated PDFs, which must not be assumed current.
- Internal links: `/practice-tests/combination-vehicles/`, `/blog/cdl-class-a-b-c/`.
- Guardrail: manual-based test preparation, not a substitute for supervised hands-on training; do not invent a universal operational sequence.

### 3. Tanker Liquid Surge: Baffles, Bulkheads, and Outage

- Primary query: `tanker endorsement liquid surge`.
- Proposed slug: `tanker-liquid-surge-baffles-outage`.
- Reader outcome: distinguish the concepts without relying on rote memorization.
- Outline: definitions; how different compartments affect movement; why loading conditions matter; common answer traps; three or more original practice explanations where useful.
- Original value: a comparison table of the terms and a plain-language scenario for each.
- Source lead: current commercial handbook section on tank vehicles from the [California DMV handbook portal](https://www.dmv.ca.gov/portal/driver-handbooks/), or the current manual for the selected jurisdiction.
- Internal links: `/practice-tests/tanker/`, `/blog/cdl-endorsements-explained/`.
- Guardrail: do not invent loading percentages, braking distances, or exact exam question counts. Verify all safety explanations.

### 4. Passenger vs School Bus CDL Endorsements: P and S

- Primary query: `passenger vs school bus endorsement`.
- Proposed slug: `passenger-school-bus-endorsements-p-s`.
- Reader outcome: see the different study topics and understand which official requirements to check for their intended work.
- Outline: comparison table; typical testing topics; training-verification checkpoints; vehicle restrictions and state-specific considerations; examples; practice-test links.
- Original value: a study-path checklist, distinguishing endorsements from CDL class and restrictions.
- Source leads: [FMCSA drivers, endorsements and restrictions](https://www.fmcsa.dot.gov/registration/commercial-drivers-license/drivers), [FMCSA ELDT](https://www.fmcsa.dot.gov/registration/commercial-drivers-license/entry-level-driver-training-eldt), plus the relevant state's current rules.
- Internal links: `/practice-tests/passenger-bus/`, `/practice-tests/school-bus/`.
- Guardrail: do not recycle expired school-bus waivers or present special exemptions as general rules.

### 5. Failed an Illinois CDL Test? Plan Your Next Attempt

- Primary query: `failed CDL permit test Illinois`.
- Proposed slug: `illinois-cdl-test-retake-study-plan`.
- Reader outcome: locate the current Illinois retake policy and build a targeted study plan instead of immediately repeating the same mistakes.
- Outline: identify the failed test; official retake-policy summary; what to verify before booking; mistake log template; practice routine; what happens if knowledge is strong but skills need work.
- Original value: a reusable error log and a weak-topic study plan.
- Source leads: [Illinois CDL/CLP information](https://www.ilsos.gov/departments/drivers/drivers-license/cdl/cdl.html), [Illinois official CDL study guide](https://www.ilsos.gov/publications/pdf_publications/dsd_cdl10.pdf). Verify current versions at writing time.
- Internal links: `/practice-tests/general-knowledge/`, `/practice-tests/`, `/blog/how-to-pass-cdl-written-test-first-try/`.
- Guardrail: do not generalize Illinois waiting periods to other states. Do not promise a passing result or imply practice replaces mandatory training.

## How to improve the priorities with real data

When available, use Search Console queries/pages to find impressions with weak clicks, related unanswered queries, and pages competing for the same intent. Compare by country, device, and time window; use Keyword Planner or another trusted dataset for volume estimates and label the source/date. Keep a topic ledger of primary query, intent, existing target URL, draft IDs, source freshness, publish date, impressions, clicks, and practice-test starts.

Review outcomes weekly. Update or consolidate an existing page when that best serves learners. Five new drafts a day is a capacity target, not an SEO ranking signal.
