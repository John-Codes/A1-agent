# Texas CDL Air Brakes Practice: What to Check Before the Test

Air brakes can feel complicated until you connect each part to the safety job it performs. Use this short guide to review the air-brakes knowledge topics before your Texas CDL test.

## Build a simple inspection habit

Start with the air-pressure gauges and warning devices. Confirm that pressure builds normally, the low-air warning activates when pressure is low, and the tractor protection system is ready. During a pre-trip inspection, look for damaged hoses, loose fittings, and leaks. A leak can reduce braking power and delay the response of the brakes.

## Remember the safe sequence

Follow the Texas CDL manual and your examiner's instructions for the exact test sequence. Learn why you perform each step instead of memorizing a list without understanding it. Check the parking brake, service-brake response, and air-pressure range required by the vehicle. If a warning appears, stop and identify the problem before moving the vehicle.

## Practice with confidence

Read the official Texas CDL manual, explain each check aloud, and practice on the same type of vehicle you will test in. This guide supports study; the official manual and examiner instructions control the test.
